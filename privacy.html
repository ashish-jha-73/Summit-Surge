This policy explains what information the Summit Surge game ("the game", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Ikshvaku (contact: ikshvaku73@gmail.com).

The game can be played in two ways:

  • Offline mode. Your progress (coins, gems, characters, maps, best runs, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Online mode (ages 13 and over). You sign in with Google, choose a username and enter your age, so that your progress is kept in the cloud and your best run can appear on the world leaderboard.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, photos, contacts or payment details. The game has no in-app purchases.

Ads

The game shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to revive or to earn a few coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings.

Every player is asked their age once, when the game first starts. Players who say they are under 13 are never shown ads, and the ad SDK is not used for them; they can reach the same rewards in other ways. Ads for everyone else are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Who processes your information

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores online accounts and progress. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob serves ads. Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell your information. Apart from the services above, we share it with no one unless required by law.

Children

Summit Surge can be played by everyone. Players under 13 can only play offline: no account is created, no progress leaves their device, and they see no ads. If you believe a child under 13 has created an online account, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Online account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the game: Settings → Delete account. Your profile, progress and leaderboard entry are removed immediately.
  • Without the game: follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Offline progress is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored by Google Firebase. Our database rules let each player read and change only their own account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, or to object to its use. Email ikshvaku73@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
