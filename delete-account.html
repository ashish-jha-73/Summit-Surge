Delete your Summit Surge account

Summit Surge by Ikshvaku

Home Privacy policy

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Summit Surge and tap the Settings (gear) button.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the game

Email ikshvaku73@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Summit Surge account" and include your in-game username. Send it from the Google account you signed in with so we can confirm it is yours. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record (Firebase account)
  • Your username and age
  • Your progress: best runs, totals, coins, gems, unlocks and items
  • Your entry on the world leaderboard

Nothing is kept afterwards. Progress saved on your own device in offline mode stays there until you uninstall the game.
